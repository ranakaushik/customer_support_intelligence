# Acme Retail: support policies (MOCK, for this project only)

These are the only policies the draft reply may quote. If a question isn't covered here,
the reply must say the team will check and follow up, never guess.

## Returns and refunds
- Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.
- Damaged or wrong items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.
- Support agents cannot approve refunds in a chat or email reply; refunds are approved by the refunds team after review.

## Delivery
- Standard delivery: 3–7 business days. Express delivery: 1–2 business days (metro cities only).
- If an order is more than 3 business days past its expected delivery date, the customer can request a trace with the courier.
- Express delivery fee (₹149) is refunded if an express order arrives late.

## Cancellations
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are dispatched.
- After dispatch, the order must be returned under the returns policy.

## Billing
- Duplicate or unrecognised charges are investigated by the billing team within 2 business days.
- We never ask customers for their full card number, CVV or OTP. Customers should not share them.

## Account access
- Customers can reset their password using the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
- If an account may be compromised, the security team locks it and contacts the customer within 24 hours.

## Escalation
- Escalated cases are reviewed by a senior support specialist, who contacts the customer within 24 hours.
- Product safety reports (overheating, burning smell, injury) are passed to the product safety team immediately; customers should stop using the product.

## Support hours
- Monday to Saturday, 9:00–21:00 IST.
